About Ouyang Jude

From design to delivery.

Hubei Ouyang Jude Automobile Co., Ltd. (湖北欧阳聚德汽车有限公司) specializes in the research, development, manufacturing and sales of semi-trailers. Based in Zaoyang, our products serve freight logistics and construction customers.

Our range includes flatbed, dump, fence, low-bed and container semi-trailers. We work with customers to discuss vehicle dimensions, loading requirements and configuration options.
