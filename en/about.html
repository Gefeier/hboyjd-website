About Hubei Ouyang Jude

Semi-trailer design, manufacturing, sales and service. Based in Zaoyang, Hubei, China.

Company profile

Hubei Ouyang Jude Automobile Co., Ltd.

Founded in 2017, Hubei Ouyang Jude Automobile Co., Ltd. specializes in semi-trailers and transport vehicles for freight logistics and construction applications. The company brings together research and development, design, manufacturing, sales and after-sales service.

Our Chinese company name is 湖北欧阳聚德汽车有限公司, also known as 湖北欧阳聚德 or 欧阳聚德. The romanized name is Hubei Ouyang Jude. Our official website is hboyjd.com.

What we do

A trailer range built around transport needs.

Our products include flatbed, dump, fence, low-bed and skeletal container semi-trailers, alongside special-purpose vehicles. Customers can discuss dimensions, cargo requirements and configuration options with our team.
